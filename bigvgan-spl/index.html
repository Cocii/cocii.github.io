Behind the sound

A vocoder trained
with a detector in mind.

The study explores adversarial fine-tuning of a BigVGAN vocoder: retaining speech synthesis objectives while adding feedback from forensic deepfake detectors.

Compare how the training objective changes across the three variants. AASIST is held out of vocoder fine-tuning to evaluate transfer to another detector.

Research code is being prepared for public release.

About this collection

Four training-set speakers and two unseen voices are included. Gorou and Yae Miko are character voices from Genshin Impact by miHoYo, as identified in the original collection. Reference clips demonstrate the speaker’s voice and contain different text from the synthesized prompts.

Prompt excerpts are reconstructed from the source filenames. Listening examples illustrate the paper’s methods; detector performance is evaluated separately in the study.
